From Support Desk to Data: Why I Am Becoming a Data Analyst

22 November 2026 · 3 min read

When I look back at my work, I notice something. Almost every job I have had was about numbers, records and decisions. I did not plan it that way, but the pattern is clear. This post explains how I got here and where I am going.

What my work taught me

  • Insurance: I reconciled balances, prepared daily reports and kept account records correct. One small mistake in a number can cause a big problem later.
  • E-commerce: I ran a business on eBay, Amazon and AliExpress, with nearly 9,000 listings and around 4,000 orders. I learned to watch sales performance and act on it.
  • ERP application support: I worked with company master data and Excel reports, and I helped users fix problems. I learned that clean data is the base of every good decision.
  • Live data research: At MuzFlips, I lead live data research and study ticket prices. Every day I turn raw numbers into a decision for the team.

Why a Data Analyst?

In every one of these jobs, my favorite moment was the same. It was the moment when I understood why the numbers looked the way they did. A number alone is not useful. A number with a clear reason behind it helps people decide what to do next. That is the work of a data analyst, and it is the work I want to do full-time.

My goal: become a Data Analyst who can find the story inside the data and explain it in simple words.

What I am learning now

I am building my skills in four areas, step by step:

  • Advanced Excel: to clean, organize and summarize data quickly.
  • Power BI: to turn data into clear, interactive dashboards.
  • Python: to handle larger data and automate repeated work.
  • SQL: to ask questions to databases and get exact answers.

What comes next

I will build real projects in five areas: Advanced Excel, Power BI, Python, SQL and Data Research. I will publish each one on GitHub, and I will write about it here: what the problem was, which tools I used, and what I found.

If you are hiring, or you need help with data work as a freelancer, I would be glad to talk. You can reach me from the contact section of my portfolio.

Shahadat Hossain Aspiring Data Analyst · Co-Team Leader at MuzFlips
