-- About Me --

A Problem Solver Who Loves Data

I'm Shahadat Hossain. I have a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering and 11+ years of total work experience across insurance, e-commerce, ERP application support and live data research.

I completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering while working full-time, so I am used to managing my time, learning new things and delivering results at the same time.

Today I work as a co-team leader at MuzFlips. For more than 4+ years I have led live data research, researched ticket prices and managed inventory, so I work with data every day.

Before that, I ran an e-commerce business on eBay, Amazon and AliExpress. I created nearly 9,000 listings, handled around 4,000 orders and earned about $10,000 in sales.

I am now building my skills to become a Data Analyst. I am learning advanced Excel, Power BI, Python, and SQL, and I like to understand why the numbers look the way they do before I draw a conclusion.
